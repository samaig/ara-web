The Challenge

AI is fragmented.
Users pay the price.

Multiple subscriptions. Lost context. Decision fatigue. The current AI landscape forces users to become experts just to get started.

Multiple Subscriptions

$20 for ChatGPT Plus. $20 for Claude Pro. $20 for Gemini Advanced. Users pay $60/month for a fragmented experience with no unified memory or workflow.

Decision Overload

Which model should I use for this task? Users waste mental energy choosing tools instead of creating. Every new chat requires a decision that slows them down.

Lost Context

Switching platforms means starting over. No conversation history, no learned preferences, no continuity. Context is lost with every tab switch.

🚀 Market Opportunity

The AI market is exploding, but users are overwhelmed. Multiple subscriptions, fragmented experiences, and decision fatigue create friction at every step.

Araviel eliminates this friction. We're the intelligent orchestration layer—like Stripe for payments or Twilio for communications, but for AI.

🎯 Competitive Advantage

Network effects through personalization: The more users engage, the smarter our routing becomes. We learn what works and compound that knowledge across our entire user base.

Unlike model providers, we're model-agnostic. As new models emerge, we integrate them instantly—giving users access without switching platforms.

📈 Path to Scale

B2C2B strategy: Launch with individuals, expand to families, capture small teams, scale into enterprise. Each segment builds on the previous one's network effects.

International expansion follows language support—starting with English markets, then Spanish, French, Mandarin, and Hindi.
