Our Story

Why we're building this

The Problem We Lived

We were power users. ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Gemini Advanced. We loved the tech but hated the chaos. Managing three subscriptions, losing context, never knowing which model to use.

The Idea

What if the AI could choose itself? What if there was an intelligence layer that orchestrated all models like a conductor? We sketched the Araviel Decision Engine at 2 AM. By morning, we knew we had to build it.

Our Mission

We're not building another model. We're building the bridge between all models and the people who need them. A personal intelligence system that makes AI feel less like a tool and more like a companion.
