Market Opportunity

The AI market is exploding, but users are overwhelmed. Multiple subscriptions, fragmented experiences, and decision fatigue create friction at every step.

Araviel eliminates this friction. We're the intelligent orchestration layer—like Stripe for payments or Twilio for communications, but for AI.
