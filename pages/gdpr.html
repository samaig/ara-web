GDPR Compliance

Effective Date: November 18, 2025

1. Our Commitment to GDPR

Araviel is committed to protecting the privacy and rights of individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar data protection laws.

This page outlines how we meet our GDPR obligations and how you can exercise your rights.

2. Who We Are

Data Controller: Araviel Ltd

Registered Address: London, United Kingdom

Contact: privacy@araviel.ai

Data Protection Officer: dpo@araviel.ai

3. What Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

3.1 Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Account preferences

3.2 Usage Data

  • AI conversations and queries
  • Model selection history
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Performance metrics

3.3 Technical Data

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

3.4 Payment Information

  • Billing details (processed by Stripe)
  • Payment method information
  • Transaction history

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

4.1 Contract Performance

Processing necessary to provide our AI orchestration service to you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4.2 Legitimate Interests

We process data to improve our service, prevent fraud, and ensure security (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4.3 Consent

For marketing communications and non-essential cookies, we rely on your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

4.4 Legal Obligation

To comply with legal requirements such as tax and accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. Your GDPR Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

5.1 Right to Access (Article 15)

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

How to exercise: Email privacy@araviel.ai with "Data Access Request" in the subject line.

5.2 Right to Rectification (Article 16)

You can correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

How to exercise: Update information in your account settings or contact us at privacy@araviel.ai.

5.3 Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Article 17)

You can request deletion of your personal data when:

  • The data is no longer necessary for its original purpose
  • You withdraw consent and there's no other legal basis
  • You object to processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds
  • The data was unlawfully processed

How to exercise: Email privacy@araviel.ai with "Deletion Request" in the subject line or delete your account in Settings.

5.4 Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18)

You can request we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.

How to exercise: Email privacy@araviel.ai with your specific request.

5.5 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

You can receive your data in a structured, commonly used format and transfer it to another service.

How to exercise: Request a data export via Settings → Privacy → Export Data, or email privacy@araviel.ai.

5.6 Right to Object (Article 21)

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.

How to exercise: Opt out of marketing emails via the unsubscribe link or contact privacy@araviel.ai.

5.7 Right Not to Be Subject to Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Araviel's AI routing (ADE) is designed to assist, not make legally binding decisions. If you have concerns, contact us at privacy@araviel.ai.

6. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your GDPR rights:

  1. Send an email to privacy@araviel.ai
  2. Include "GDPR Request" in the subject line
  3. Specify which right you wish to exercise
  4. Provide proof of identity (to prevent fraud)

Response Time: We will respond within 30 days (extendable to 60 days for complex requests).

Cost: Exercising your rights is free unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

7. International Data Transfers

Araviel is based in the UK, and we may transfer data to countries outside the EEA. When we do:

  • We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • We ensure adequate safeguards are in place (Article 46 GDPR)
  • We work with processors who maintain GDPR-compliant data protection standards

Third-party processors:

  • OpenAI (USA) - Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Anthropic (USA) - Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Google (USA) - Standard Contractual Clauses
  • AWS (USA/EU regions) - GDPR-compliant infrastructure

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Active accounts: Duration of your subscription + 30 days
  • Closed accounts: 90 days for recovery, then deleted
  • Conversation history: Retained while your account is active; deleted upon account deletion
  • Payment records: 7 years (legal requirement for tax purposes)
  • Marketing data: Until you opt out or 2 years of inactivity

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Encryption at rest (AES-256)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls and authentication (MFA available)
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Incident response procedures

10. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms:

  • We will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours (Article 33 GDPR)
  • We will inform affected individuals without undue delay if the breach poses a high risk (Article 34 GDPR)
  • Notifications will include the nature of the breach, likely consequences, and measures taken

11. Children's Privacy

Araviel is not intended for children under 16 in the EEA. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental consent. If you believe we have collected data from a child, contact us immediately at privacy@araviel.ai.
