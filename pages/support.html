Frequently Asked Questions

Account & Billing

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.

Can I change my subscription plan?

Yes! Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan. You can upgrade anytime (effective immediately) or downgrade (effective next billing cycle). No penalties for changing plans.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Navigate to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. You can reactivate anytime before the period ends.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer prorated refunds within 30 days of subscription purchase if you're not satisfied. Contact support@araveil.ai with your account details and reason for cancellation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) and debit cards through Stripe. Enterprise plans can be invoiced with payment via bank transfer.

Using Araveil

How does the Araveil Decision Engine (ADE) work?

ADE analyzes your query's intent, complexity, and requirements, then routes it to the best AI model in real-time. It considers factors like task type, model performance, availability, and your preferences.

Can I choose which AI model to use?

Pro and Team users can manually select models or set preferences for specific task types. However, ADE generally provides better results through automatic routing.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Your conversations are encrypted and stored securely. We never use your data to train third-party AI models unless you explicitly opt in. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I export my conversation history?

Go to Settings → Privacy → Export Data. You'll receive a JSON file with all your conversations within 24 hours. You can also export individual conversations from the conversation menu.

What's the difference between models?

Different models excel at different tasks. GPT-4 is great for code and analysis, Claude 3 for creative writing, Gemini for research. ADE automatically picks the best one for each query.

API & Integration

How do I get an API key?

API access requires a Pro or Team plan. Once subscribed, go to Settings → API → Generate New Key. Store it securely - it won't be shown again.

What are the API rate limits?

Pro: 10,000 requests/month (100/minute). Team: 50,000 requests/month (200/minute). Enterprise: Custom limits. See the API Reference for details.

Can I use Araveil in my commercial product?

Yes! All paid plans include commercial use. Team and Enterprise plans are designed specifically for integrating Araveil into your products and services.

Do you have SDKs for different languages?

We provide official SDKs for Python and JavaScript/Node.js. Community-maintained SDKs are available for Ruby, Go, PHP, and more. See the API documentation.

What happens if I exceed my API rate limit?

You'll receive a 429 error. Your limit resets monthly (for total requests) and per-minute (for burst limits). You can upgrade your plan for higher limits.

Technical Issues

Why are my responses slow?

Check the System Status page for outages. Some models are slower than others - ADE balances speed and quality. For faster responses, you can prefer faster models in Settings.

I'm getting an "Authentication Error"

This means your API key is invalid or expired. Generate a new key in Settings → API, or log out and back in if using the web interface. Ensure you're including the "Bearer" prefix in API requests.

My conversation history isn't saving

Ensure you're logged in (not guest mode) and Private Mode is disabled. Check that your subscription is active. If the problem persists, contact support.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@araveil.ai with: (1) What you were trying to do, (2) What happened instead, (3) Any error messages, (4) Your browser/device info. Screenshots are helpful!

Where can I see service uptime?

Visit our System Status page for real-time uptime monitoring, incident reports, and scheduled maintenance notifications.
