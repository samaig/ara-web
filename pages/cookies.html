Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our service.

2. How We Use Cookies

Araviel uses cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you interact with our platform
  • Improve our service performance and reliability
  • Analyze usage patterns and optimize features
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

Required for core functionality

  • Authentication: Keep you logged in across sessions
  • Security: Protect against fraudulent activity
  • Session Management: Maintain your active session state

These cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the service to function.

3.2 Functional Cookies

Enhance your experience

  • Remember your language preference
  • Store your theme settings (light/dark mode)
  • Save your AI model preferences
  • Remember conversation history settings

You can control these cookies through your account settings.

3.3 Analytics Cookies

Help us improve Araviel

  • Track which features are most popular
  • Measure page load times and performance
  • Understand user navigation patterns
  • Identify areas for improvement

We use services like Google Analytics and PostHog. You can opt out of these cookies.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

Used for advertising and outreach

  • Track conversion from marketing campaigns
  • Personalize advertising content
  • Measure effectiveness of email campaigns
  • Retarget visitors with relevant ads

You can opt out of marketing cookies through your preferences.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We work with trusted third-party services that may set their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics and performance monitoring
  • Stripe: Payment processing (essential for subscriptions)
  • Intercom: Customer support chat
  • PostHog: Product analytics and feature usage
  • Cloudflare: CDN and security services

These services have their own cookie policies. We recommend reviewing their policies:

  • Google Cookie Policy
  • Stripe Cookie Policy

5. Cookie Duration

Cookies can be:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them

Our Cookie Retention Periods:

  • Authentication cookies: 30 days
  • Preference cookies: 1 year
  • Analytics cookies: 2 years
  • Marketing cookies: 90 days

6. Managing Your Cookies

6.1 Through Araviel Settings

You can control non-essential cookies in your account settings:

  1. Log in to your Araviel account
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy
  3. Toggle cookie preferences on/off

6.2 Through Your Browser

Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Note: Blocking all cookies may prevent you from using Araviel's core features.

6.3 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • Your Online Choices (EU)

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Currently, there is no industry standard for responding to DNT signals. We respect your privacy choices and provide opt-out mechanisms for analytics and marketing cookies.

8. Cookies and Your Privacy

We are committed to protecting your privacy:

  • We never sell cookie data to third parties
  • Cookies are encrypted and secure
  • We minimize data collection to what's necessary
  • You can request deletion of your cookie data at any time
  • We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology or regulations. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or a notice on our website.
