Our Story

Built by People Who Believe
AI Should Feel Human

The Problem We Lived

Like many of you, we were early adopters. We subscribed to ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, and Gemini Advanced. We toggled between tabs constantly, copying prompts, losing context, never quite sure which AI would give us the best answer.

We were spending more time managing our AI tools than actually using them. The promise was simplicity. The reality was chaos.

The Moment Everything Changed

One late night, debugging a stubborn piece of code, we had an epiphany. What if the AI could choose itself? What if there was an intelligence layer above the models, orchestrating them like a conductor leading an orchestra?

We sketched the first prototype of what would become the Araviel Decision Engine on a whiteboard at 2 AM. By morning, we knew we had to build it.

Why Araviel

The name comes from a blend of "AI" and "ravel," meaning to untangle or clarify. We're here to untangle the complexity of AI and make it feel simple, warm, and human.

We're not trying to build the next model. We're building the bridge between all of them and you. A companion that grows with you, learns from you, and makes AI feel less like a tool and more like a partner.

Who We Are

We're engineers, designers, AI researchers, and product thinkers who've worked at companies like Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Stripe. We've seen the cutting edge of AI, and we know what's possible.

But more importantly, we're users. We're writers, developers, parents, students, and dreamers who want AI to enhance life without adding complexity. We're building Araviel for ourselves, and for everyone who feels the same way.

What Drives Us

We believe AI should be accessible, warm, and trustworthy. We believe transparency matters more than hype. We believe the best products don't feel like products at all. They feel like extensions of yourself.

That's what we're building. Not just software. A companion. A bridge. A new way to think, create, and grow.
